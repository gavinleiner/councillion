# Fourteen whole food, sport and nature concepts

These inventories add fourteen real questions, with 464 independently written familiar inputs and 38 wrong-category boundaries. Source rows, spelling variants and product variants remain answers within their whole concept. They do not add extra questions. Normal-play admission is explicit in `admission.json` and remains separate from source generation.

## Named foods

The full retained named-food lists remain the starting point. `food-admission-repairs.json` supplies explicit scope, identity, name and reference decisions; `authored_food_repairs.py` applies them after source reconciliation. Source list parents that accidentally join multiple cereal names are held: Flahavan's Progress Oatlets and Kavanagh's Porridge remain separate names, as do Curves Fruit & Nut Crunch and Curves Honey Crunch. A joined parent string is not an additional cereal product.

[King Arthur's Madeleine Cookies](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/vanilla-madeleine-cookies-recipe) establishes ordinary cookie usage for madeleines. [Amaretti Virginia](https://www.amarettivirginia.com/en/) supports the generic amaretti family, which does not become the same identity as the specific Amaretti di Saronno variety. [Girl Scouts](https://www.girlscouts.org/en/cookies/cookie-flavors.html) supports the actual cookie names; singular serving names are explicit aliases, never generated initialisms. Digestive biscuit and familiar cookie styles retain common tiers.

The pie/tart/flan scope deliberately includes dishes conventionally called pies, including the cake-based [Boston Cream Pie](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/boston-cream-pie-recipe) and [Whoopie Pie](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-chocolate-whoopie-pies-recipe), just as it includes mash-topped or crumb-based pies. This is an explicit culinary convention, not a claim that those two foods have pastry crusts. Ordinary cake, pizza and frittata remain excluded. [Suvretta House's own bakery](https://suvrettahouse.ch/en/shop/walnut-tart) supports Engadine nut tart/Bündner Nusstorte; the earlier broad-source hold does not override that specific evidence.

[Cold Stone](https://www.coldstonecreamery.com/icecream/signaturecreations/birthdaycakeremix/) and [the manufacturer's Chubby Hubby product record](https://smartlabel.unileverusa.com/076840100125-0001-en-US/index.html) support the named ice-cream additions. [Nestlé's cereal recipes](https://www.nestle-cereals.com/my/my-en/recipes) and [granola products](https://www.nestle-cereals.com/my/my-en/brands/granola) support the broadened breakfast-cereal scope, including hot and unbranded cereal kinds. Muesli/Mueslix and Granola/Granula remain distinct valid names; one must not be accepted as the other through spelling correction. Historical cereal products remain eligible.

[TABASCO's barrel process](https://www.tabasco.com/blog/about-our-barrels/) and the [producer collaboration describing fermented pepper mash](https://www.absolut.com/en/blog/vodka-and-spirits/introducing-absolut-tabasco/) support Tabasco as an explicit alias of Tabasco sauce in the fermented-food inventory. [King Arthur's beignets](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/classic-beignets-recipe) and [kolaches](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/kolaches-sweet-savory-recipe), along with [Kikkoman's spring-roll account](https://www.kikkoman.eu/food-stories/detail/spring-rolls-vs-summer-rolls), support the named pastry repairs. Beignet and Italian bignè remain separate preparations.

[J NTO's udon/soba guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/ph/guide/udon-soba/) supports conventional menu preparation names; spaghetti, udon and soba are explicitly eligible in the noodle-preparation scope. Bare unfilled pasta shapes such as fusilli remain outside that dish question. Rabokki is the actual rice-cake-and-ramen dish; the invented canonical label “Tteok-bokki noodles” is removed. Plain tteokbokki is not silently treated as noodles. Leaf-parcel spellings Khao tom mat/mud and Patoleo/Patholi share scoring identity. Traditional wrapping and documented leaf cooking qualify; merely setting another food on a leaf plate does not.

## Sports and nature

The motorsport inventory uses [FIA](https://www.fia.com/sport), [FIM's documented disciplines](https://www.fim-moto.com/en/fim/history-heritage) and [UIM](https://www.uim.sport/). It explicitly includes recognized racing series and powered-vehicle competitions. Rally/trials public names are aliases; driver or manufacturer names and unpowered sports are not disciplines. Bodyweight movements use the [ACE exercise library](https://www.acefitness.org/resources/everyone/exercise-library/) and [NHS exercise references](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/exercise/strength-exercises/). Natural leg extension and reverse Nordic curl are one movement identity.

Horned/antlered animals include documented horn-like structures and named groups with qualifying members; owl feather tufts, tusks alone and fictional unicorns are excluded. Underground crop kinds include documented edible roots, bulbs, tubers, corms, rhizomes and underground fruits, not any above-ground fruit simply because its plant has roots. These retain their zoological/horticultural references in the question records.

[Bermuda's Department of Environment and Natural Resources](https://environment.bm/bermuda-fireworm) supports Bermuda fireworm/Odontosyllis enopla as bioluminescent. Light-producing bacterial symbiosis counts; fluorescence or bright color alone does not. [FAO's Desert Locust Guidelines](https://www.fao.org/ag/locusts/common/ecg/347_en_DLG1e.pdf) and [Australian government locust accounts](https://www.agriculture.gov.au/biosecurity-trade/pests-diseases-weeds/locusts/about/migratory) support recurring migration names. Leatherback turtle and leatherback sea turtle share identity. Migrating populations count; one-off escapes do not establish migration.

These are finite source-backed inventories with explicit editorial boundaries, not proof that every possible food variation or species has been enumerated. Rarity remains an editorial familiarity estimate. The requested 5,000-question release is unfinished.
