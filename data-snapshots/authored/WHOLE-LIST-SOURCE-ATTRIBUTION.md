# Whole playable name lists

Six questions cover cheese, flatbreads, pancake dishes, stews, browsers and cloud forms. The source sublists, alternate spellings and 2,980 answer identities are not extra questions.

The factual name inventories in `sources/whole-playable-lists.json.gz` were retrieved on 2026-10-25. Wikipedia name-list adaptations are shared under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Exact source pages are retained with each group and question. No photographs, article descriptions or images are reproduced as game assets. Cheese extraction follows the global list and its linked national/type lists into one inventory. It excludes Irish producer tables, reference/reading sections, and non-cheese sections of the redirected Turkish cuisine page. `whole-list-holds.json` records additional raw labels excluded from play.

The [WMO International Cloud Atlas classification table](https://cloudatlas.wmo.int/en/cloud-classification-summary.html) and [cloud genera](https://cloudatlas.wmo.int/en/clouds-genera.html) supply factual classification terms. Named genera, species, varieties, features and established common cloud terms are answers; no Cartesian multiplication of Latin labels creates extra answers or questions. WMO photographs and explanatory prose are not included.

Browser publisher pages confirm ordinary product names rather than treating a rendering engine or website as a browser. Food supplements link to recipe publishers and named product manufacturers. The explicit specifications preserve independent common inputs, negative boundaries, canonical-name merges and source links. Those checks do not establish that all imaginable recipe combinations are enumerated, or that editorial rarity tiers are measured player frequencies.

The NOAA/NWS fog guide and weather glossaries supplement the atmospheric inventory with named fog forms and conventional cloud terminology. Explicit public phrases such as cumulus cloud and condensation trail are preserved; unrelated digital-cloud brands remain excluded.
