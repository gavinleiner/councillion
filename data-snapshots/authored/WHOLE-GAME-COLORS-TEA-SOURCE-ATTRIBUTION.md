# Whole game inventories, tea brands and color names

Reviewed October 27, 2026. These six independently chosen whole subjects expand normal play by six questions. The original review's approachable whole-category model supplies the editorial standard; source entities, language rows, implementations and list headings never create extra questions.

Pokémon data derives from the retained English PokeAPI CSV tables, under the captured BSD license in sources/pokeapi-license.txt. Sources and source IDs are retained in sources/pokemon-whole-inventories.json.gz. All English moves, abilities and item labels are reconciled; repeated game/implementation labels share an answer. Official old move names remain aliases. Types include the eighteen standard types plus Stellar, with unknown placeholders and spin-off-only Shadow held. The broader item/trainer-device prompt also includes Pokédex, Rotom Phone, Pokétch, Pokégear, PokéNav, C-Gear and P★DA, supported by the sources recorded per question, including official Pokémon Rotom material and Nintendo's Pokémon Pearl manual. Pokédex is not falsely represented as a bag-item table row. Software-only DexNav is outside the stated device scope. No inferred initials are accepted.

Tea brands use the complete captured List of tea companies source, explicit row/heading reconciliation in tea-brands-review-ledger.json, and primary brand catalogs in sources/tea-primary-source-manifest.json. Names count as tea brands/companies, including recognizable bottled teas. Parent owners alone are not brands. Lyons and T2 duplicate rows are merged, source footers are held, and genuine public short names remain aliases.

Colors use the full captured alphabetical named-color list and all 148 W3C CSS Color 4 named-color keywords: https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#named-colors . Sources are retained in sources/color-name-source.json.gz. The answer inventory is a list of color names, not copied color descriptions or illustrations. Publisher/version qualifiers do not make the same ordinary color name multiple answers. Regional Gray/Grey and established synonyms share identity. The review ledger records source/identity decisions, with Navy merged into Navy blue after the candidate-stage capture.

Editorial point tiers represent familiarity estimates, not measured player frequencies. Common inputs are separately enumerated and tested through the actual game matcher. Wrong-category boundaries remain explicit. The global typo matcher was not loosened to hide source omissions.
