# Open whole-concept factual inventories

`sources/open-knowledge.json.gz` records factual Wikidata names, aliases, typed memberships, source queries and sitelink counts. Wikidata structured facts are [CC0](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing). QLever is the read endpoint, not independent editorial approval. No Wikipedia article prose, musical recordings or performer images are included.

The admitted music questions cover record labels, real DJs/DJ acts, real drummers and keyboard instruments. `sources/performer-classification.json` preserves a separate audit of non-human occupation records and their types. Fictional story performers are excluded by entity identity. Documented live stage acts remain eligible: TAMA's Nyango Star artist profile and Martîn Rongen's own performance biography supply explicit exceptions to misleading source classifications. A tour or band is not an individual drummer. Billed real DJ duos are eligible DJ acts. Supplemental omissions are verified against performer sites, instrument manufacturers, broadcasters or the Radio Hall of Fame; exact links are in the specifications. Public stage names such as II and ATB and actual labels such as ECM and A&M are retained explicitly. No initials are manufactured from longer names. Three otherwise unusable label names have explicit Latin transliterations of their recorded names; the original source labels are preserved.

`sources/world-fiction-classification.json` separately checks fictional classification across the relevant world/object source roots. The canals question accepts named artificial waterways, not natural rivers and straits. The waterfalls question includes named complexes; its supplemental checks link to UNESCO, the Krka park authority, the waterfall operator and NPS. Entity/name counts do not count as extra questions. Pending deserts, airports, castles, archaeological sites and burial sites remain candidates; their unresolved issues are recorded separately.

Editorial rarity uses familiar input checks and linked-source prominence, with explicit uncommon anchors. It is not a claim of measured player response frequencies. The snapshots and common-name sweeps do not warrant a promise that every possible name or source assertion is infallible.
