# Fairy/folk tales and literary genres/forms

This batch supplies **two whole questions**, not one question per tale, genre,
country, title word, table row, tag or alias. It follows the saved review's
familiar whole-category standard. Original independent familiar probes: 49
fairy/folk titles and 45 literary/written forms. Neither answer volume nor
source authority terms count toward the 5,000-question publication target.

The reproducible compiler is `scripts/prepare-authored-story-forms.py`. Its
specifications, source selection, identity/alias policies, held rows, canonical
source map and request receipts are frozen alongside this document. Editorial
rarity tiers describe expected familiarity; they are not measured frequencies.

## Material review decisions

- Tale authors, anthology titles, source countries and ATU plot numbers never
  become answers merely because they occur in the source tables. Shared ATU
  numbers, a shared character article or a shared anthology link do not merge
  distinct named tales. The Happy Prince and The Nightingale and the Rose stay
  different despite both source links pointing to the collection article.
- Grimm's Snowdrop is an alternate name for Snow White. Andersen's The Snowdrop
  is a different flower story. The leading-article distinction is deliberately
  retained; no blanket article-dropping alias rule is generated. Snow White and
  Rose Red, Pretty Goldilocks and the Gingerbread Horse are different tales.
- The Andersen capture labels one porcelain-figure story "The Shepherdess and
  the Sheep." The body and independent published translations establish the
  Chimney Sweep title. The erroneous Sheep label is held, not accepted as an alias.
- All eleven Frost chapter entries are held pending standalone-tale proof. The
  primary book is a connected narrative, not proof that every chapter is an
  independent tale. The composite Adventures of Chanticleer and Partlet heading
  is held rather than equated to its three different component Grimm tales.
  Bedside Tales of Sultan is a collection, not an individual fairy tale.
- The writing source has bare context labels such as Hard, Science, Medical and
  Space, sidebars, methods, religious study fields and named scriptures. Actual
  full forms such as Hard fantasy, Hard science fiction, Medical romance and
  Medical journalism remain different. Bare adjectives, individual titles,
  unsupported redlinks and subject-only branches are not admitted as genres.
- Novelette is separate from novella, graphic short story/novella from graphic
  novel, and progression fantasy from LitRPG. UF authority references help find
  public names but are not automatically game-identity merges.
- Original unfamiliar vernacular tale titles and legitimate rare written forms
  remain in the answer tail. They do not turn into ultra-specific questions.
- Captured HTML hashes, indexed-only evidence, failed requests and a live
  Gutenberg Canada library-card shell are separately recorded. No library login
  was attempted. That shell supplies no primary story text evidence.

This is broad documented coverage, not a claim that every tale or written form
in human history has been identified. New source rows remain subject to role,
identity and familiar-answer checks before being admitted.
