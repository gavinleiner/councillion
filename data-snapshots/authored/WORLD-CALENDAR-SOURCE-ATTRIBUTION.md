# Awards and calendar observances: two whole questions

Batch 38 adds **two questions**, not one prompt per source item, category,
country, year, prize recipient or calendar date. The independent proposal retains
all 35 original familiar award inputs and all 46 original holiday inputs.
The reviewed banks contain 1,793 canonical award answers and 359 calendar answers.
Aliases are spellings of an answer, not additional questions or scoreable answers.

The frozen specifications, source rows, policies and review ledger are the
inspectable factual and identity inputs. `prepare-authored-world-calendar.py`
reproduces both banks; its check mode rejects unreviewed name/alias changes.
The usual admission gate separately decides whether either question enters play.

## Sources and eligibility

The public indexed bodies of Wikipedia's film, literary, sport, music,
television, mathematics, civil and game award lists were read with section and
line context. They are not all-anchor lists: festival/association names,
recipients, sport names, medalist directories, navboxes, source headings,
unsupported labels and events without an identified prize are held. The
unverified unlinked “minor music awards” stanza does not approve its own names.
The full source-directory page is a directory, not thousands of award identities.
416 source leads are held. Actual named families and named award categories,
including the 24 category headings in the Academy's 98th ceremony record,
are answers inside one broad award question.

First-party public names and omissions were checked against the Academy,
Television Academy, Recording Academy, Tony Awards, BAFTA, Nobel Foundation,
Pulitzer administration, ALA, Carnegies, Carnegie Hero Fund, IMU, Abel Prize,
ACM, Lasker Foundation, Peabody, PDSA, Heisman, NHL, Pro Football Hall of Fame,
Silver Slugger, Army/Navy, UK Ministry of Defence, House of Representatives,
YouTube, Ig Nobel, Golden Joystick, Game Awards, Academy of Interactive Arts & Sciences, and actual
award sponsors. The source receipts distinguish captured HTML from indexed
public text and failed access; no raw capture is claimed for an indexed read.
Where an individual primary URL was unavailable, a recorded public list/name
proof remains the basis rather than the failed request being treated as proof.

Holiday rows come from the reviewed holiday-list body, all actual name cells
in UNESCO's complete calendar, and named OPM, UK, Canadian, Singapore and Hong
Kong public holidays. Smithsonian, census, religious-calendar and festival
publishers support familiar omissions and translations. Calendar dates,
months, weekdays, people, deities, explanatory prose and annual campaign themes
are not automatically holiday names. 24 source leads are held, and headers
are removed before the lead inventory. Government substitute dates do not
create a new holiday answer for every year. An official regional holiday is
not asserted to be a public holiday everywhere.

The holiday prompt explicitly includes **traditional** festivals and established
calendar observances. Modern stand-alone concert/film festivals and conferences
are a separate subject. This scope clarification preserves every original
familiar input, including Carnival, Mardi Gras and Oktoberfest. Named observance
periods and documented unofficial observances count; arbitrary marketing names
do not become valid solely by being plausible.

## Identity decisions

- The Carnegie Medals for writing, illustration and civilian heroism are distinct.
  The former Kate Greenaway Medal is the illustration medal; bare Carnegie Medal
  resolves to the writing medal. Czech and Austrian Kafka prizes remain distinct.
  A misplaced source-country entry pointing to the Czech prize is held; the
  Austrian prize has separate actual publisher proof. Australian and American
  People's Choice awards also remain distinct.
- The Polish Nautilus award differs from the Nautilus Book Awards. Rea's short
  story award differs from the CWA Short Story Dagger. Bare first-link labels do
  not remove the rest of an award's name; Harvill Secker's named translators'
  prize is not the publisher itself.
- The Astounding award's historical Campbell name is not the Campbell Memorial
  award for a novel. Otherwise/Tiptree and genuine translated award titles share
  identity. Oscar/Academy Award, real public singular/plural names and actually
  published short names are explicit aliases. No initialisms are generated.
- Gold Glove and Golden Gloves, Booker and International Booker, different
  Nobel categories, and the different Lumière prizes remain distinct.
- Diwali and Bandi Chhor Divas, Halloween and Samhain, May Day and Beltane,
  Christmas and Yule, Veterans Day and Remembrance Day, Boxing Day and St Stephen's
  Day, and Day of the Dead and All Souls' Day are not joined by date or ancestry.
  Nowruz's documented public spellings and formal international-day name share
  the spring-festivity identity; the Baha'i Naw-Ruz observance stays separate.
- Actual translated/public names work, including Deepavali, Hari Raya Puasa/Haji,
  Dia de Reyes, Ching Ming, Tuen Ng, Vaisakhi/Baisakhi and Hanukkah spellings.
  Ordinary Labor/Labour spelling shares a named holiday identity. It does not
  merge every May holiday with the North American September observance.

## Access and scoring limits

Raw root/list and some publisher requests returned 403, other errors or redirects;
those receipts are retained and were not evaded. The Hong Kong root is an index;
its linked 2026 schedule was separately captured. The guessed Smithsonian spotlight
URL returned 404, so the actual American History museum resource is used. The
USCCB Triduum URL returned 403, so independent Church of England calendar proof
supports the corresponding observed periods. The Cannes URL redirected to an
actual festival report that explicitly awards the Palme d'Or; it is not described
as a capture of a modern complete prize catalogue. Public indexed text is not
misrepresented as a raw downloaded document. No login, external browser automation
or privacy-control workaround was used.

Only factual names, reviewed membership and aliases are shipped, not articles,
award artwork, festival imagery or publisher descriptions. Wikipedia references
remain identifiable and its source material is available under CC BY-SA 4.0;
primary publishers retain their own content rights. The development research
captures are distinct from the shipped factual bank.

Rarity uses explicit editorial familiarity tiers, not measured player frequencies
or answer length. Common entry names score 10, other original familiar names 30,
recognizable extras 60, the documented rare tail 85, and the independently chosen
rare anchors Dickin Medal and Parwanaya 100. These are not exhaustive claims about
every unpublished award, every local observance or future calendar changes.
The release remains unfinished until 5,000 validated questions are published.
