# Recorded-song catalog sources

These 26 artist questions use whole factual song lists, not artist/album/year/tag combinations. The saved user review is the playability model; these are new editorial admissions, not additional user Yes votes.

The source snapshot retains factual titles, source table sections, performer/release fields, held rows, retrieval date and the source HTML SHA-256. It contains no lyrics, audio, cover art or copied narrative descriptions. Wikipedia source table text is available under CC BY-SA 4.0; source URLs and individual page histories provide attribution. These snapshots and edited factual inventories retain that attribution. Individual song titles and factual recording relationships are used for trivia.

The parser expands row/column spans, extracts quoted titles without guest credits or legend symbols, and excludes explicit unreleased sections and writing-only or unrelated performer credits. Source redirects to biographies or singles-only pages are not treated as complete catalogs. Credited cast performances are included where the artist song source includes that performance. All 379 independently written recognizable inputs must match exactly; negative album and performer-credit boundaries must fail. Remixes, live versions and repeated versions share one underlying scoreable title. Real alternate/short titles are explicit; no initials are generated. Rarity is an editorial approximation, not measured player answer frequency.

A primary-publisher recording repairs Taylor Swift’s own Better Man, which was missing after the older Little Big Town table-row credit was filtered. Background-only Lady Gaga rows, Maroon 5 solo-member features and the Weeknd’s unnamed-title placeholder are held. Linkin Park fan-contest performances by different artists are held.
