# Culture inventories

This batch contains seven whole-subject questions. None is multiplied by a
release year, title word, country, paired tag, or a slight rephrasing.

Factual structured names, explicit aliases, occupation/title/type claims and
sitelink counts come from Wikidata (CC0), retrieved October 25, 2026 through
QLever. The exact source queries and the extra film-type eligibility query are
preserved in `sources/culture-whole-concepts.json.gz`. Genre claims alone did
not admit people, franchises or television series as movies. Film identity
checks removed 19 non-film source records before compilation.

Supplementary factual title lists (no prose, descriptions, plots or images):

- [List of heist films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_heist_films)
- [List of time travel works of fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_time_travel_works_of_fiction), Film section only
- [List of films featuring time loops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_films_featuring_time_loops)
- [List of fictional schools](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fictional_schools)

Credit the contributors and linked page histories under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The derived
factual inventories using these lists retain that attribution and share-alike
license. Individual factual names and editorial prompts are not copied prose.
The extraction records preserve the original titles; bibliography authors,
real universities and descriptions are not silently turned into school names.

Primary checks and supplements include Disney's official Princess lineup,
DC's Batman and Riddler profiles, the Star Wars Databank, the Warner Bros.
Hogwarts model description, Pixar's Monsters University description, Sony's
Baby Driver and Groundhog Day descriptions, and Marvel's Avengers: Endgame
page. Specific links are retained beside each question in the source batch.

The saved independent challenge file checks common guesses separately from
source membership. For example, Mulan belongs to the official Disney Princess
lineup; the flux capacitor is a vehicle component; Captain Amelia is a ship's
captain and not automatically a pirate. The corrupted English source label for
Hogwarts is replaced with its documented name and is not retained as an alias.
Explicit synonyms such as X-Mansion / Xavier's school and 12 Monkeys / Twelve
Monkeys share one identity, rather than rewarding a duplicate answer.

This is an incremental content batch. Its answer rows do not count as extra
questions and do not complete the requested 5,000-question expansion.
