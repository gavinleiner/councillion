# Whole hotel and online-community subjects

Retrieved and reconciled October 27, 2026. These are **two questions**. The
hotel inventory has 539 canonical names and the community inventory has 499;
answer names, source rows, portfolios and historical aliases do not add questions.
The independent proposals specified 104 hotel guesses and 91 community guesses
before either source inventory was compiled. They are not generated from a list
and do not prove that every possible answer has been found.

## Sources and reproducibility

`sources/brands-platforms-list-sources.json.gz` retains complete captured list
sections with source URLs, line numbers and context. The public encyclopedia
lists of chained-brand hotels, defunct hotel chains, social networking services
and defunct social networking services supply broad current/historical coverage.
Their text is available under Wikimedia's CC BY-SA terms. The retained Corpora
social-network list is CC0: https://github.com/dariusk/corpora . These are candidate
facts to reconcile, not an automatic assertion that every listed item qualifies.

Primary hotel portfolios from Hilton, Marriott, IHG, Accor, Hyatt, Choice,
Louvre, Sonesta, Yotel, Extended Stay America, Frasers Hospitality, Strawberry,
Leonardo and Karisma fill omissions. Actual public lists, primary publisher
names, paraphrased name-history proofs and research receipts are preserved in
`sources/brands-platforms-public-captures.json.gz`. Full publisher marketing copy
is not redistributed in that archive. Every question's references
and every canonical answer's original source rows remain inspectable.

`brands-platforms-review-policies.json` records semantic holds, source-label
repairs, actual public names and identity merges. The compiler
`scripts/prepare-authored-brands-platforms.py --check` reproduces batch 30 and
the review ledger from those retained inputs. It does not fetch changing pages
during a build. Some public pages returned 403, 404 or empty JavaScript shells;
they were not retried with evasion. Indexed public publisher evidence and retained
whole-list facts supply the applicable names. Curious Cat's historical community
role additionally has a retained public app-catalog record; no claim about its
precise closure date is needed to answer the question.

## Boundaries and identities

The hotel prompt includes real past/current lodging chains, branded collections,
serviced apartments and primarily hotel-operating groups. It excludes individual
independent properties, booking marketplaces, private vacation-home rental
services and unrelated investors. Denizen and the canceled Marriott Nickelodeon
concept never operated. Undergraduate by Hilton's first stays are announced for
2027, after this snapshot, so it is held. The separate operating Karisma
Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts brand counts. Generic footer locations, corrupted
link labels and combined table cells cannot silently become answers.

Source misspellings such as Grand Millenium are corrected rather than registered
as exact spellings. Metropolo's primary name replaces a translated source label.
Comfort Inn and Comfort Suites stay distinct. Actual brand replacements such as
AmeriSuites/Hyatt Place, Summerfield Suites/Hyatt House and Jurys Inn/Leonardo
share an answer. Clarion Collection's documented Home Hotel rebrand shares an
identity. Corporate acquisitions alone do not automatically merge every brand.

The social prompt includes community sites, social virtual worlds and named group
communication services. Ordinary email, office spreadsheets, individual profiles,
learning-management software, dedicated dating/adult services and unverified old
stubs are outside its explicit scope. A site's audience identity is not a reason
to exclude it: general LGBTQ community sites remain eligible. Historical services
still count. Actual public X, QQ, VK, AIM, ICQ and Hi5 names are allowed; fabricated
initials such as FB/IG/YT are not introduced.

Twitter/X, Musical.ly/TikTok, Spot.IM/OpenWeb and Yammer/Viva Engage share
identity. Weixin and WeChat remain separate interoperable services, as Tencent
explicitly describes; Douyin/TikTok and Kuaishou/Kwai also remain distinct apps.
Facebook and Messenger are separately named products. The Chinese nickname for
Bilibili is not registered as an alias that the current ASCII normalization would
collapse to a single B. No global spelling tolerance has been relaxed.
