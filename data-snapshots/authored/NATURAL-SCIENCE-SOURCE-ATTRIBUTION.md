# Nature and academic whole concepts

Eight independently specified whole subjects were reviewed October 27, 2026. The complete lexical hyponym closures for the selected WordNet 3.0 roots are retained in sources/natural-science-wordnet.json.gz, with sense IDs, source aliases, definitions and hypernyms. The WordNet license is retained at ../variety/wordnet-license.txt; see https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use and https://wordnet.princeton.edu/citing-wordnet . Princeton University does not endorse Councillion.

A dictionary classification is evidence to examine rather than automatic eligibility. True-fungi inventories exclude modern slime molds, oomycetes and clubroot organisms. Reptiles exclude mythical hoop snakes and non-mammalian synapsids. Academic fields exclude discredited pseudosciences and relation/technique/action senses that are not disciplines. Definitions support preserving legitimate psychometrics and biological reflexology senses rather than excluding them by a misleading everyday association. Humanities and social-science disciplines are explicitly in the broader academic prompt's scope.

Public and scientific synonyms share one supported answer: hen-of-the-woods/maitake, porcini/Boletus edulis, slow worm/Anguis fragilis, eastern newt/red eft, queen conch/Strombus gigas, pistol/snapping shrimp, deer tick/Ixodes scapularis/Ixodes dammini, and hooded tickspider/ricinuleid. Distinct asp/cobra species, whip spider/whip scorpion and neurology/neuroscience remain separate. Actual ambiguous public names have an explicit eligible owner; arbitrary initials are not generated.

Primary scientific and institutional evidence recorded with the inventories includes the Field Museum's non-mammalian synapsid collection, the Australian Museum's reptiles, AmphibiaWeb, the US National Park Service's eastern newt factsheet, NOAA Fisheries' queen conch, the World Register of Marine Species, the American Arachnological Society's Ricinulei, the World Arachnida Catalog, NCBI taxonomy and the AMS/zbMATH MSC2020 classification. Modern familiar kinds omitted by the older lexical source are explicit editorial records. Scientific names and source descendants add answer coverage, never extra prompts. The rare anchors are reviewed lexical examples; scores are editorial familiarity estimates, not measured player frequencies.

Sources, explicit holds, independent familiar probes, genuine synonym merges and canonical counts are inspectable in natural-science-specifications.json, natural-science-holds.json and natural-science-checks.json. Classification does not claim that this lexical snapshot is every current species on Earth; ordinary recognizable names must still be tested and repaired rather than rejected because the source is old.
