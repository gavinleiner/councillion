# Familiar fictional worlds

SpongeBob SquarePants, Toy Story and Shrek/Puss in Boots are three whole questions. Film, episode, season, surname and source-section variants do not add questions. The separate Avatar candidate remains outside normal play pending complete novel/cast reconciliation.

The retained full named Wikipedia character lists are supplemented with the character/cast fields from Buzz Lightyear of Star Command, The Adventures of Puss in Boots and Puss in Boots: The Last Wish. Only factual names and explicit source name fields are retained, not article descriptions. Snapshot receipts, canonical labels, independent familiar inputs, held organizational headings and exact alias decisions remain inspectable in `sources/friendly-culture-names.json.gz` and `friendly-culture-specifications.json`.

Primary familiar-character references include [Nickelodeon's SpongeBob overview](https://www.nick.com/info-page/xe31fl/spongebob-squarepants), [Pixar's Toy Story](https://www.pixar.com/toy-story), [Toy Story 5](https://www.pixar.com/toy-story-5), [Disney's Toy Story 4 character guide](https://www.disney.co.uk/movies/toy-story-4/meet-the-characters), [Mattel's licensed Benson product](https://service.mattel.com/us/productPopup.aspx?prodno=GGJ89&siteid=27), [DreamWorks' Shrek](https://www.dreamworks.com/movies/shrek) and [the studio's Last Wish screenplay](https://pibfyc.dreamworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/PUSS-IN-BOOTS_THE-LAST-WISH_Screenplay.pdf). The last source explicitly identifies Ethical Bug as its own parody character, so Jiminy Cricket is not falsely accepted as the same character. Secondary character/episode records support Kevin C. Cucumber, Fuzzy Acorns and Fifi omissions; exact references are attached to the questions.

Public names Plankton, Karen, Larry, Patchy, Potty, Neptune, Squilliam, Squidina, Burger Beard, Andy, Bonnie, Lotso, Sarge, Bo, Al, Duke, Pricklepants, Potato Head, Puss and Kitty are explicit aliases. SpongeBob's Quickster persona, Squidward's Captain Magma, Patrick's Elastic Waistband and Sandy's Miss Appear share their actual character identity. Young Goldilocks shares Goldilocks' identity, and Death/Wolf is one character. Named individual children in a combined source field are retained individually. A source paragraph accidentally beginning “The Raenoks are…” is not an answer; the actual named Kleev record remains. Chlorm scientists Era, Eon and Epoch are retained separately from the malformed combined heading.

The questions deliberately include established collective character identities, such as the Toy Story Aliens and the Three Little Pigs. Their scopes state this. Voice actors, places, organizational headings and generic species alone are excluded. Actual public nicknames do not widen the global spelling matcher or create guessed initialisms. Rarity tiers remain editorial estimates, not measured player frequencies.

These are complete retained source lists with explicit supplemental and identity decisions. They are finite snapshots, not a claim that every character in every future episode or licensed work has been enumerated. The 5,000-question release remains unfinished.
