# Bird families and computer/space lists

The admitted addition contains eight whole questions: six bird groups, computer languages and astronauts. The separate government-space-agency inventory remains a candidate pending complete organization/name classification. Names, species, breeds, source tables and aliases do not add question counts.

## Six bird groups

`familiar-bird-families.json.gz` retains every species in the Cornell eBird/Clements Anatidae, Picidae, Trochilidae, Laridae, Hirundinidae and Ardeidae families from the previously captured complete taxonomy response. The source snapshot date is October 21, 2026; preparation of the six derived whole inventories is October 26. All 17,891 raw taxonomy rows are accounted for by response SHA256 `d5bfbb7bc0824258869fa828c98ac0560759ac2a022d06ffa3e928ce0841b308`. The six eligible family lists contain 174, 236, 363, 105, 92 and 74 species respectively. Scientific and English names share a scoreable species identity.

Sources: [Cornell checklist downloads](https://www.birds.cornell.edu/clementschecklist/download/) and [eBird taxonomy](https://science.ebird.org/en/use-ebird-data/the-ebird-taxonomy). These are factual names and taxonomy membership only, not copied species accounts or images.

The complete retained named domestic duck and goose breed lists expand the single Anatidae inventory. [The Livestock Conservancy](https://livestockconservancy.org/heritage-breeds/conservation-priority-list/) provides primary familiar-breed support; Wikipedia list receipts and two explicit unverified breed holds remain in the snapshot. Pekin, Indian Runner, Khaki Campbell and Embden/Emden names are explicit. Graylag/Greylag, Gray/Grey Heron and the public Green Woodpecker name remain accepted. Old split-species names are recorded deliberately, including [Red-rumped Swallow](https://science.ebird.org/cs/use-ebird-data/the-ebird-taxonomy/2024-ebird-taxonomy-update); current species keep distinct identities. The gull/tern question explicitly includes skimmers, because the complete Laridae family includes them. Unrelated puffins, swifts, storks and hummingbird hawk-moths remain outside the corresponding groups.

## Computer languages

The retained complete alphabetical language list is reconciled with [GitHub Linguist's programming/markup classifications](https://github.com/github-linguist/linguist/blob/main/lib/linguist/languages.yml), explicit document/XML vocabulary lists and validated prose-markup entries. File extensions and syntax-highlighter aliases are not automatically public language names. Named XML vocabularies and document-markup formats are explicitly eligible; organizations, individual elements, generic data models and platforms are held. JSON and plain English are deliberate wrong-category boundaries for this scope.

[W3C XML](https://www.w3.org/XML/), [Markdown's original project](https://daringfireball.net/projects/markdown/), [Microsoft Visual Basic](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/visual-basic/), [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/), [Blockly](https://developers.google.com/blockly) and the [INTERCAL implementation/manual](https://catb.org/~esr/intercal/) support familiar omissions or unusual language identities. Full-name/acronym pairs such as HTML/HyperText Markup Language and XML/Extensible Markup Language share one scoring identity. Actual names such as C, C++, C#, R and Go remain distinct; guessed initialisms are not created.

## Astronauts

The retained worldwide alphabetical list is supplemented by [NASA's full 2025 candidate class](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/astronauts/astronaut-candidates/), with nine missing names added and Anna Menon's existing identity retained. Selection for professional astronaut training is explicitly eligible even before a flight. NASA's former/active lists and [fact book](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/astronaut-fact-book/) support the scope.

Primary NASA/ESA biographies support actual public nicknames, including Gus Grissom, Deke Slayton, Pete Conrad, Gene Cernan, Butch Wilmore and Tim Peake. Full first-and-last forms are derived only from the person's actual name; conflicting names retain deterministic ownership. Fictional characters and people not selected as professional astronauts are rejected. The source lists, actual aliases and independent familiar cases remain inspectable beside the specifications. This finite snapshot is not a universal completeness claim.
