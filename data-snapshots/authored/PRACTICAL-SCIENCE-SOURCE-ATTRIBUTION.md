# Seven whole practical-knowledge questions

This development batch contains **seven questions**, with 1,953 canonical answers
and 201 separately specified familiar inputs. A term number, named subtype,
respirator certification, instrument model family or puzzle variant is an answer
inside its question. None adds a prompt. The requested 5,000-question release
remains unfinished.

## Sources and scope decisions

**Anatomy.** FIPAT, *Terminologia Anatomica*, second edition 2.07 (2019;
adopted by IFAA in 2020), [Part II](https://cdn.dal.ca/content/dam/dalhousie/pdf/library/FIPAT/TA2/FIPAT-TA2-Part-2.pdf)
and [Part IV](https://cdn.dal.ca/content/dam/dalhousie/pdf/library/FIPAT/TA2/FIPAT-TA2-Part-4.pdf).
The publications explicitly identify their individual terminology terms as public
domain. The PDF publications themselves carry CC BY-ND 4.0. The stored source
rows retain term numbers and PDF page numbers, British/American names and real
synonyms. Public everyday names supplement the formal terminology.

The joint chapter also contains ligaments, membranes, discs, synovial fluid and
cavities. Those are not automatically joints. The vessel section also contains
heart chambers, vessel-wall layers, lymphatic-only structures, valves and closed
fetal ligaments. Those are not blood vessels. Actual fetal vessels remain valid.
Recognized vessel groups and arterial/venous networks remain in scope.
The selection ledger records these distinctions rather than copying every row.

The same joint cannot score again under an equivalent term: examples include
ellipsoid/condyloid, synovial/diarthrosis and gomphosis/dentoalveolar syndesmosis.
Different named vessels such as left and right coronary arteries remain distinct.
IVC, SVC, LAD, PDA and TMJ are explicit published public abbreviations; there is
no general initials generator. A source misspelling of “interphalangeal” is
repaired, without installing the wrong spelling as an exact alias.

**Weather.** The full text of WMO-No.8, seventh edition, was retained from its
[public National Weather Service distribution](https://www.weather.gov/media/epz/mesonet/CWOP-WMO8.pdf).
Its age is explicit; it is not presented as the current edition. Additional public
instrument names and observation platforms are checked against the
[NWS glossary](https://www.weather.gov/otx/Full_Weather_Glossary),
[NOAA PSL instrument catalogue](https://psl.noaa.gov/data/obs/instruments/SurfaceMetDescription.html),
[Met Office radiation instruments](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/guides/observations/how-we-measure-sunshine)
and [Singapore NEA instrument descriptions](https://www.nea.gov.sg/corporate-functions/resources/publications/annual-reports/annual-weather-review-2011/weather-instruments/).
Historic nephoscopes and modern remote sensors count. Ordinary umbrellas and
unrelated vehicles do not. A source typo calling a humidity recorder a
“hydrograph” does not create that exact alias for a hygrograph.

**Protective equipment.** The list draws on
[OSHA's PPE overview](https://www.osha.gov/personal-protective-equipment),
[OSHA's equipment examples](https://www.osha.gov/sites/default/files/enforcement/directives/CPL_02-00-154.pdf),
[HSE's equipment categories](https://www.hse.gov.uk/ppe/using-the-right-type-of-ppe.htm),
[CDC's nine filtering-facepiece classes](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/ppe/respirators/ffr.html),
[NIOSH's named respirator types](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/NIOSH-CEL/Information/Terms),
[Health Canada's international names](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-medical-devices/masks-respirators/information-users.html)
and the [CDC's international-class examples](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/prevent-getting-sick/types-of-masks.html).
N95 and KN95 are different named classes, not aliases of one another. SCBA and
PAPR are actual published names; arbitrary two-letter inputs are not inferred.
Lab coats and lead aprons are included. Sunscreen is explicitly listed as PPE by
[Safe Work Australia](https://www.safeworkaustralia.gov.au/safety-topic/managing-health-and-safety/personal-protective-equipment-ppe/overview);
barrier creams appear in OSHA's examples and are retained. A generic PFD and a
life jacket are not collapsed into one identity. This question classifies names
and makes no claim that an item is suitable for a particular hazard.

**Electricity.** The main inventory uses
[EIA's generation account](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/how-electricity-is-generated.php),
[DOE's solar explanation](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/how-does-solar-energy-work),
[DOE's fuel-cell types](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/fuels/types-fuel-cells),
[Natural Resources Canada's MHD research](https://natural-resources.canada.ca/science-data/science-research/research-centres/low-temperature-magnetohydrodynamics-power-conversion-system)
and [NASA's conversion technologies](https://technology.nasa.gov/advancing-commercial-space/power_systems).
Specific research checks include the
[microbial-fuel-cell report](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/13-Microbial_Fuel_Cells.pdf),
[demonstrated pyroelectric conversion](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41563-018-0059-8),
[betavoltaic devices](https://www.nasa.gov/general/autonomous-tritium-micropowered-sensors/)
and a [demonstrated solar thermophotovoltaic system](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19990028453).
Sources, devices and conversion processes are all explicit in the prompt.
Electrochemical/storage devices count without claiming that stored energy is a
new primary energy source. Experimental conversion is not described as a
commercial power plant. Perpetual-motion and fictional generators are excluded.

**Radiation.** Primary references include
[NASA's electromagnetic spectrum](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/),
[EPA's radiation categories](https://www.epa.gov/radiation/radiation-basics),
[ITU's frequency nomenclature](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-V.431/en),
[NASA's radiation budget](https://science.nasa.gov/ems/13_radiationbudget/),
[Smith–Purcell/transition experiments](https://www1.psfc.mit.edu/research/wab/pdfs/2006/Marsh%20AIP%20877.pdf),
[cyclotron detection](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.162501)
and [CERN's radiation reference](https://geant4.web.cern.ch/documentation/pipelines/master/prm_html/PhysicsReferenceManual/electromagnetic/xray_production/InvSynFracInt.html).
Physical processes, named bands and particle-radiation types are included within
one question. The prompt explicitly limits its scope to electromagnetic and
particle radiation. It does not reject gravitational or acoustic radiation as
fictional; they are outside that scope. Hawking radiation is explicitly a
theoretical prediction, not an asserted direct observation.

**Puzzles.** Names and families are checked against
[Nikoli's catalogue](https://www.nikoli.co.jp/en/category/en/),
[Grandmaster Puzzles](https://www.gmpuzzles.com/blog/),
[the Dalgety/Hordern mechanical-puzzle classification](https://www.puzzlemuseum.com/class/puz-class-2-classification.htm),
[Rubik's products](https://www.rubiks.com/products),
[WCA's named puzzle forms](https://www.worldcubeassociation.org/regulations/),
[Puzzle Baron](https://www.puzzlebaron.com/puzzles/)
and [Simon Tatham's complete forty-form catalogue](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/puzzles/).
Real alternate form names share identity: Hashi/Hashiwokakero,
Light Up/Akari, Tentai Show/Spiral Galaxies, Hidoku/Hidato and Keen/KenKen.
Generic games such as Fortnite do not qualify merely because they contain a
puzzle. “Arithmogriph” is held pending a precise source/identity decision.

## Reproduction and limits

`practical-science-specifications.json` preserves the independent familiar-input
checks and explicit editorial scoring decisions. Scoring is not measured player
frequency and does not depend on spelling length. The compressed source rows,
scope-selection ledger, synonym policy and compiler reproduce the batch offline.
The shared matcher is unchanged. Corrections still require submission first.

The source-receipt file records successful captures and failed/blocked retrievals.
Public indexed text was usable for several official pages whose direct download
failed. No browser challenge or OS privacy control was bypassed. The PDFs and
full source-text research remain saved in the local continuation workspace.
Bibliographic references document classifications and names; they are not claims
that one source exhaustively lists every possible answer. Further valid names
can be added when supported. The count above concerns the actual curated bank,
not source pages, aliases or a claim of perfect coverage of an open universe.
