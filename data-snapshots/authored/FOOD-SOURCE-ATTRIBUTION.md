# Food-list source attribution

The factual name snapshot in `sources/food-name-lists.json.gz` extracts names from the English Wikipedia articles linked in its `pages` records: **List of cookies**, **List of pies, tarts and flans**, **List of pastries**, **List of breakfast cereals**, and **List of fermented foods**. Attribution: contributors to those English Wikipedia articles. Retrieved October 25, 2026.

Wikipedia source articles are available under **Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International**, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>. That source license and attribution are preserved for the extracted source compilation and corresponding source-derived food inventories wherever copyright applies. Individual factual food/product names are identifiers; no source recipes, prose descriptions, photographs or page layouts are included.

Changes: extracted names, repaired flattened labels, merged documented synonyms, held questionable category members and unusable labels, added separately reviewed familiar examples, and assigned original game points. Page URLs and unresolved source entries remain inspectable in the snapshot, question references, and food-source-holds ledger. Unrelated site code and other separately licensed datasets retain their own licenses.
