# Two whole food and science subjects

These inventories add TWO questions: name a chili/bell-pepper type, or name a natural human hormone/neurotransmitter. Their 321 canonical answer identities do not add 321 questions. The 62 familiar input probes were proposed before the source inventories. This development batch does not complete the requested 5,000-question publication.

## Peppers

The [New Mexico State University cultivar account](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_circulars/CR706/index.html) supplies its whole historical release table and accompanying breeding accounts. The table combines New Mexico 6/6-4, while the prose documents the 1957 breeding change; those selections stay distinct. Original and Heritage releases, Sandia/Sandia Select and Española Improved/Española Select likewise stay distinct. Pod types and release years add no prompts.

The [Chile Pepper Institute public seed category](https://chilepepperinstitute.ecwid.com/Seeds-c85441005) was captured across both ordinary pages, all 116 products. Its two multi-cultivar assortments and Jalapeño Spice Mix are not single cultivar identities; four onion products are outside Capsicum. Both ordinary fallback detail pages (Padron and Red Caribbean Habanero) are accounted for as catalog-name evidence only, without invented descriptions. NuMex PWB is a pepper. CaJohns/CaJohn's Jumbo Serrano, Las Cruces/Las Cruces Cayenne and published naming variants share identity. Hontaka, Santaka and Takanotsume are not merged by resemblance.

The [Fedco pepper catalog](https://fedcoseeds.com/seeds/peppers) was captured through page two, all 56 products. Organic/ECO/conventional packaging shares cultivar identity; blends do not become individual types. Crop/category suffixes are removed from display names. Habanada is not Habanero; the source's Habanada species claim is not reused as taxonomy.

[University of Melbourne plant names](https://www.plantnames.unimelb.edu.au/Sorting/Capsicum_annuum.html) and its four other cultivated-species pages support explicitly selected public names. They are not blanket modern synonym authorities. [Mexico's agriculture ministry](https://www.gob.mx/agricultura/articulos/ahora-es-cuando-chile-verde-le-has-de-dar-sabor-al-caldo) and [CICY's field/cuisine publication](https://www.cicy.mx/Documentos/CICY/sitios/CircuitoEtnobiologico/Repositorio/Publicaciones/2021_Chile_Mole_Chilmole_Catalogo_Recetas.pdf) support fresh/dried Jalapeño/Chipotle, Poblano/Ancho, Chilaca/Pasilla and Mirasol/Guajillo relationships. Round Cascabel and Guajillo remain distinguishable named types despite regional name overlap. Mulato's distinct source-described type remains separate. NuMex Mirasol is not Peruvian Ají Amarillo.

Creator/grower sources supply actual [Carolina Reaper](https://puckerbuttpeppercompany.com/pages/faq), [Pepper X](https://puckerbuttpeppercompany.com/pages/our-roots), [Naga Viper](https://www.cercatoridisemi.com/en/product-page/naga-viper-capsicum-chinense), [Chocolate Habanero](https://southdevonchillifarm.co.uk/products/chocolate-habanero), [Scotch Bonnet](https://southdevonchillifarm.co.uk/products/fresh-scotch-bonnet-chillies) and [Bird's Eye/Chili Padi](https://www.chengtainursery.com/product/chili-padi-m150/). No current hottest-pepper record is asserted. Product names, factual identities and source hashes are retained; marketing prose and artwork are not redistributed. Real named color cultivars count; arbitrary generated color-plus-pepper names do not.

## Human chemical messengers

[OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/17-2-hormones), sections 17.3–17.10 and 12.5, supports familiar hormone and neurotransmitter names. Only factual names/relationships and independent editorial annotations are frozen; textbook prose and illustrations are not reproduced. The source's current preface describes its CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 licensing. Table membership is not blanket eligibility: cholecalciferol is not active calcitriol, and a precursor/subunit is not its mature hormone.

The [UniProt Consortium](https://www.uniprot.org/), [license](https://www.uniprot.org/help/license), provides the frozen 98 reviewed human records returned by the Hormone keyword query, under CC BY 4.0. Query URL, content hash, total98 and absence of further pagination are retained. Original source JSON is preserved separately from the selected gameplay facts. Selected mature products are explicit; other keyword-associated records remain accounted for in the review ledger. This is not a claim that 98 genes mean 98 hormones. GFRAL is a receptor; transthyretin a carrier; thyroglobulin and POMC precursors; hormone subunits, neurophysins, copeptin and inactive NT-proBNP are not automatically answers. Asprosin/Placensin are not aliases for their structural fibrillin parents. Arbitrary numbered fragments do not enter automatically.

The [Society for Endocrinology public index](https://www.yourhormones.info/browse/) supplies named hormones; [Kisspeptin](https://www.yourhormones.info/hormones/kisspeptin/) supports Metastin. Separate checks include [DHEA sulfate](https://www.medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/dhea-sulfate-test/), [Estetrol](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Estetrol), [Vanderbilt neurotransmitters](https://vkc.vumc.org/topics/neurotransmitters), [Orexin/Hypocretin](https://www.southtees.nhs.uk/services/pathology/tests/hypocretin-orexin/), IUPHAR/BPS ligand accounts, Health Canada endogenous cannabinoids, NCBI MeSH ATP and the cited neurophysiology text. These add real endogenous messengers, not synthetic-only analogues or drug brands.

DHEA/DHEAS, Cortisol/Cortisone, TSH/TRH, T3/T4/rT3, NPY/PYY, alpha/beta CGRP and individual Orexin subtypes remain distinct. Estrogen is a named class, not an alias collapsing Estradiol/Estrone/Estriol. Actual conventional public names GABA, ADH, TSH, ACTH, GH and GLP1 are explicit. Initials are not generated; unnecessary obscure two-letter shortcuts are omitted. English Alpha/Beta/Gamma qualifiers prevent ASCII normalization from erasing subtype identity. Medication brands are not aliases, while an actual natural chemical name such as Hydrocortisone remains valid even when also used in medicine.

## Scope and scoring

The selection and held ledgers record limitations. These are recognizable subjects with entry answers and a real rare tail; they are not complete universes of every historical cultivar or proposed signaling molecule. Rarity is editorial familiarity, not measured player frequency or spelling length. Explicit familiar/entry lists determine tiers; NuMex LotaLutein and Kisspeptin are separately chosen rare anchors. Source errors, missing descriptions and unresolved roles do not become invented facts.

Some direct URLs returned 403 or a TLS error. Those were not bypassed or treated as captured documents. Separately accessible indexed primary text was used where stated. Production publication remains separate from research-record counts.
