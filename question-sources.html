Questions, sources & coverage

Each prompt uses a finite, downloadable answer bank. A broad prompt may have thousands of answers; aliases and alternate spellings count as the same answer. We retain the long tail of source names instead of accepting only the popular examples.

These are source snapshots, not a claim to contain every possible answer in the world. New releases, missing source classifications and alternate names can require updates. Dates and platform restrictions in a prompt define its scope. Community tags describe Steam categories; a game outside Steam is not eligible for a Steam-specific prompt.

The normal-play pool

Daily, Puffer and random Practice share 227 individually admitted questions across nine subjects, including all 98 Yes-voted tryout prompts. Unreviewed source variants, actor-credit joins, historical team rosters, title-word filters and multiple-label Steam questions stay out of random play. Subjects and formats are randomly balanced before drawing an unseen question.

The production build checks 1,257 familiar exact answers and 137 deliberately wrong inputs. These checks catch common omissions and bad corrections; they do not claim exhaustive coverage. The requested 5,000 genuinely different questions remain unfinished. The old 5,000-entry source archive is available only by explicit choice. Answer points estimate familiarity from source popularity, career history and selected known names. They are editorial estimates, not percentages measured from players. The 100-point discovery answer is a deterministic pick from a category’s least familiar tier. Published player names are recognized directly. Shared surnames can resolve to one valid athlete; full names distinguish the people.

New source credits: DBpedia and Wikipedia contributors (factual screen credits, CC BY-SA 4.0); OpenFlights (carrier names, ODbL); ByMykel CSGO-API (base weapon facts, MIT); retained WordNet, sports, game and music inventories. Expansion source metadata and limits.

Repeat prevention

Daily follows a shared, dated schedule: the same seven questions in the same order for everyone. Every Daily from October 25 has seven different subjects. October 25 was explicitly repaired after a reported content problem; earlier dates stay fixed. The current balanced calendar runs through November 17, 2026. New approved content can append future dates. It stops when the finite schedule runs out rather than looping. Puffer combines the participants’ question histories and carries them through rematches and host changes. History is stored per account in this browser; changing devices or clearing site data can reset that history.

Expansion sources · October 23, 2026

  • SteamSpy public tag API and Steam tag documentation: full named memberships for 197 selected tags, fetched no faster than once per second. Current names reconciled by app ID with Fronkon Games Steam dataset, revision 6a31fcb (MIT). No screenshots or store descriptions included.
  • PlayMyData, Rubei et al., November 2023: worldwide game names, platforms and genres, CC BY 4.0. Console banks reflect this retained snapshot, not releases added since then.
  • Asaniczka’s TMDB movie dataset, curator version 1097, retrieved October 23, 2026, ODC Attribution. Recent genre, studio and release facts supplemented by Evan Christiawan’s 2010–2025 film dataset (CC BY 4.0). Only released, non-adult records with at least one vote. This product uses TMDB factual metadata but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. No posters, reviews or plot summaries copied.
  • TVmaze, complete show index fetched through page 383. TV names, genres, premiere dates and original network/channel facts, CC BY-SA 4.0. The modified TV factual snapshot and derived discovery and group TV banks are distributed under the same license. Network questions refer to original broadcaster/commissioner, not every service that currently streams a show.
  • Maharshi Pandya’s Spotify tracks dataset, revision 635b034, BSD license: names and broad genre associations only. No audio or lyrics.
  • nflverse and roster dictionary: annual rosters 1920–2026. Includes AFL, offseason and practice-roster membership; does not imply a regular-season appearance.
  • nba_api all-player and team indices, plus Justinas NBA player data (NBA.com, 1996–2023) and historical NBA season facts (1950–2017). NBA team questions explicitly end at 2022–23; position-history questions end before 2018. Current NBA and WNBA name banks use the full index.
  • Sean Lahman Baseball Database, via Rdatasets / Lahman, through 2025: actual appearances, franchises, positions, Hall of Fame, All-Star selections and awards. This modified factual subset follows the Lahman database’s CC BY-SA terms.
  • J. Fjelstul World Cup Database: squad, position and winner facts for men’s 1930–2022 and women’s 1991–2019 tournaments. Those boundaries are stated in the prompts; later squads are not silently claimed.
  • Chester Ismay / ModernDive olympicAthletes 0.5.10, Olympedia and R. Griffin’s Olympic_history: athlete-event facts through the 2026 Winter Games, CC BY 4.0. Medal eligibility is checked within the requested sport.
  • Team names checked against the NHL, NBA and MLB directories. Names only; no league or team logos used.
  • Princeton WordNet: reduced noun-kind vocabularies used for explicit title-word questions; retained license and copyright notice.
  • NHL official statistics: all-career regular-season skater and goalie names, complete returned indexes retrieved October 23, 2026.
