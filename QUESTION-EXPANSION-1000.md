Each category represents 9.9–12.5% of the pool. New questions use whole subjects: recognizable game and television characters, artist catalogs, foods, sports winners and skills, wildlife, science, history and everyday objects. They add no tag intersections, title-word variants or city-by-country filler. Distinct questions—not aliases, source entities, alternate spellings or unpublished drafts—are counted toward the target.

The 321 additions contain **35,065 canonical answer entries**, including familiar entry points and less common valid answers. Familiarity scores are editorial estimates, not measured player frequencies. Each question has a deliberately selected 100-point answer. Live/remix/remaster versions of a song share one scoring identity; mixed-set excerpts are held. Primary names, established nicknames, alternate character identities and documented aircraft/car model codes remain exact inputs. Generated initials are not admitted.

The retained source references and compressed evidence cover official game registries, publisher cast guides, Apple artist/release catalogs, sports award records, park and plant references, WordNet definitions and other recorded inventories. Membership review removes award nominees from winner lists, mixed-up plant names, unrelated character crossovers and other documented scope errors. The existing NBA-coach and Soundcore inventories also receive factual/alias repairs.

Admission checks run **15,883 familiar exact inputs and 2,036 wrong-answer boundaries** through the production matcher. These checks and source review do not establish that every possible answer worldwide has been included. In particular, documented real words that resemble other valid answers remain explicit rejection boundaries: Goat milk cannot become Oat milk, and Earthworm cannot become Heartworm. The global correction thresholds are unchanged.

The published Daily dates through November 3 retain the same ordered IDs. Future dates begin drawing from the enlarged pool on November 4; the calendar currently has 132 complete shared days through March 3, 2027. Each day contains seven subjects from seven different categories. Calendar exhaustion stops rather than wrapping old questions. Puffer keeps its balanced randomized selection and played history; the new selection epoch refreshes unplayed turns in older rooms without changing the active question, hearts or turn.
