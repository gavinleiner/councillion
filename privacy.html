Councillion privacy & data

Councillion is a Future Counsel trivia game, maintained by Gavin Leiner. Contact: gavin@theleiners.com.

Google sign-in

Daily, Practice and Endless can be played as a guest. Puffer Party requires Google sign-in. Google supplies your basic profile identifier, name and email. Councillion checks Google's signed identity token and displays your name in this browser. It does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google API access. Firebase Authentication verifies the Google sign-in and stores its persistent session in this browser so you remain signed in after closing and reopening the site. Firebase refreshes short-lived authentication tokens automatically. Councillion does not keep raw Google identity tokens in its own browser storage. Sign out on shared devices; private browsing or clearing site data can remove the session. No Google client secret is included in the game.

Game progress

Daily results, settings and local profile statistics are stored on your device. Daily, Practice and Endless keep their full progress on this device. Completed-game achievement summaries can sync with your Google account as described below. Your chosen nickname, buddy and room visibility preference are saved separately for each Google account on this browser. They do not sync across devices. Sign-out removes the persistent authentication session and leaves local preferences and game progress intact. To delete all local progress, clear site data for gavinleiner.github.io in your browser settings.

Puffer careers, banners and live answer boxes

Completed Puffer matches store your Firebase user identifier, placement, win or loss, rating change, scores, accepted-answer counts, streaks and response times. These records are used to calculate your career stats, earned badges and rank. Your selected earned title, title color and three stat trackers sync to your Google account. Other signed-in players can see your Puffer career and banner. These records contain no Google email. Records begin with the ranked update; earlier results were not recorded.

During a match, everyone in your room sees the answer-box text you type and delete, including before your turn. This is disclosed before joining. Only the Puffer answer box broadcasts drafts; chat drafts and other fields do not. Drafts are temporary, expire from display after 12 seconds without a connection update, and are removed on leaving or the end of a match. They are not copied into permanent match records. Avoid typing personal information. Like the game itself, completed results are reported by the room host and are not an independently refereed competitive service.

Achievements across game modes

Completed Daily and Practice dives record the date, total score, accepted-answer count, rare-answer counts and whether all seven answers were valid. Endless records correct answers, streaks and genuine five-answer challenge clears; the secret shortcut does not count as a challenge clear. These numeric summaries unlock titles across the game and can appear in the three stats you choose to show other players. They do not contain the text you typed or the questions you answered. Guest summaries stay in this browser and are claimed once by the next Google account that connects. Signed-in summaries sync to that account; repeated Daily completion does not add another day. Other Google-authenticated players can read your individual achievement summaries to display earned titles and stats. Solo summaries are reported by your game client and do not change your Puffer rating. Clearing site data removes unsynced guest summaries; synced summaries remain with your account.

Question history

Puffer stores revealed question identifiers in this browser under your Firebase identity to avoid repeats. Joining a room shares those question identifiers with its host and room members, and the room retains them across rematches and host changes. These reports contain no previous answers or room codes. Clearing site data removes local history; it is not synchronized between devices. A fixed published schedule keeps Daily prompts from repeating within that schedule.

Moderation

Display names use 1–16 Latin letters, numbers and basic punctuation. Accents are simplified. The game and database reject names matching blocked slurs or abusive terms, including common spaced and numeric spellings. Court chat also filters blocked language. This automated filter is limited and can miss new evasions. Hosts can remove disruptive players using the chat roster.

Assigned titles and owner controls

Two designated verified Google accounts receive an assigned title. The game owner has exclusive owner titles and can inspect active public or private rooms, manage room visibility, remove players, end matches without ranking changes, and close rooms. The owner can trigger short cosmetic character or room effects, which display an owner-effect label and expire after eight seconds. An assigned Vice President title gives no additional permissions. Public title records contain a Firebase user identifier and title selection, not an email address. Account assignments are checked against verified Google identity claims by the database.

Party rooms and hosting

Online parties use Google Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database. Your chosen display name, buddy, room membership, readiness, chat messages, submitted answers, hearts, and scores are stored in the room and shared with its players. Public room codes, host display names, seat counts, and game phase appear in the directory for signed-in players. Room data may remain in the database after a game; the public listing expires when its host stops updating it. Firebase stores your Google account identifier and basic profile for authentication. Daily and Endless progress stays on your device. Your Google email and identity token are not sent to other players. Signing out or losing authorization disconnects your current party. Ordinary hourly token refreshes do not sign you out. Other players receive your Firebase user identifier, but not your Google email or sign-in token. The host controls the game state; database rules restrict membership to Google-authenticated accounts and ordinary players cannot write the host’s state. Keep personal information out of display names. GitHub Pages hosts this site and receives normal web requests. Google receives requests when you use its sign-in service. Fonts and game assets are served with this site. Councillion has no advertising or analytics tracker.

The friends leaderboard shows saved Puffer ratings, wins/losses, ranked win streaks and recent rating changes. It uses display names from shared rooms; a small account-scoped list of past opponents is remembered on this browser. It does not expose email addresses or list all Google accounts.
