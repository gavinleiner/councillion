Councillion privacy & data

Councillion is a Future Counsel trivia game, maintained by Gavin Leiner. Contact: gavin@theleiners.com.

Google sign-in

Daily, Practice and Endless can be played as a guest. Puffer Party requires Google sign-in. Google supplies your basic profile identifier, name and email. Councillion checks Google's signed identity token and displays your name in this browser. It does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google API access. Firebase Authentication verifies the Google sign-in and stores its persistent session in this browser so you remain signed in after closing and reopening the site. Firebase refreshes short-lived authentication tokens automatically. Councillion does not keep raw Google identity tokens in its own browser storage. Sign out on shared devices; private browsing or clearing site data can remove the session. No Google client secret is included in the game.

Game progress

Daily results, settings and local profile statistics are stored on your device. Google sign-in currently does not upload or synchronize them. Your chosen nickname, buddy and room visibility preference are saved separately for each Google account on this browser. They do not sync across devices. Sign-out removes the persistent authentication session and leaves local preferences and game progress intact. To delete all local progress, clear site data for gavinleiner.github.io in your browser settings.

Question history

Puffer stores revealed question identifiers in this browser under your Firebase identity to avoid repeats. Joining a room shares those question identifiers with its host and room members, and the room retains them across rematches and host changes. These reports contain no previous answers or room codes. Clearing site data removes local history; it is not synchronized between devices. A fixed published schedule keeps Daily prompts from repeating within that schedule.

Moderation

Display names use 1–16 Latin letters, numbers and basic punctuation. Accents are simplified. The game and database reject names matching blocked slurs or abusive terms, including common spaced and numeric spellings. Court chat also filters blocked language. This automated filter is limited and can miss new evasions. Hosts can remove disruptive players using the chat roster.

Party rooms and hosting

Online parties use Google Firebase Authentication and Realtime Database. Your chosen display name, buddy, room membership, readiness, chat messages, submitted answers, hearts, and scores are stored in the room and shared with its players. Public room codes, host display names, seat counts, and game phase appear in the directory for signed-in players. Room data may remain in the database after a game; the public listing expires when its host stops updating it. Firebase stores your Google account identifier and basic profile for authentication. Daily and Endless progress stays on your device. Your Google email and identity token are not sent to other players. Signing out or losing authorization disconnects your current party. Ordinary hourly token refreshes do not sign you out. Other players receive your Firebase user identifier, but not your Google email or sign-in token. The host controls the game state; database rules restrict membership to Google-authenticated accounts and other players cannot write the host’s state. Keep personal information out of display names. GitHub Pages hosts this site and receives normal web requests. Google receives requests when you use its sign-in service. Fonts and game assets are served with this site. Councillion has no advertising or analytics tracker.
