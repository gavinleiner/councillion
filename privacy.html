Councillion privacy & data

Councillion is a Future Counsel trivia game, maintained by Gavin Leiner. Contact: gavin@theleiners.com.

Google sign-in

Signing in is optional. Google supplies your basic profile identifier, name and email. Councillion checks Google's signed identity token and displays your name in this browser. It does not request Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google API access. The identity token is kept in this tab's session storage until sign-out, expiry, or the browser session ends. No Google client secret is included in the game.

Game progress

Daily results, settings and local profile statistics are stored on your device. Google sign-in currently does not upload or synchronize them. Sign-out removes the Google identity session; it leaves local game progress intact. To delete all local progress, clear site data for gavinleiner.github.io in your browser settings.

Party rooms and hosting

Online parties use PeerJS signaling to establish direct connections. Your chosen room name, answers, and scores are shared with players in that room. Keep personal information out of display names. GitHub Pages hosts this site and receives normal web requests. Google receives requests when you use its sign-in service. Fonts and game assets are served with this site. Councillion has no advertising or analytics tracker.
