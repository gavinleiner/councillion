# Court II

Court II resets **rating and placements** for every account. All-time results,
W/L, achievements, stone proofs and display preferences remain saved. Earlier
receipts remain immutable; only receipts marked `ratingSeason: "court-2"` affect
this season. Everyone begins at 0 CR and completes five new rated placements.
There is no placement multiplier, hidden starting bonus, or opponent-rating
adjustment. Five ordinary wins with the capped streak bonus reach 236 CR
(Silver); five losses finish at 0 CR (Bronze).

Consecutive positive-rated finishes add +4, +8, then +12 (capped). Any negative
finish resets this rating streak. First-place victories remain the only wins
in all-time W/L; other positive finishes are shown as PODIUM. This keeps wins
and rank progress understandable when room sizes differ.

An earned close-final-duel bonus adds +8 for the winner. The runner-up receives
+4 when their placement gains CR, or half the normal duel loss. Both finalists
must answer during a final lasting at least eight seconds and reach one heart
each. Forfeits lose at least 28 and receive no bonuses. Changes are capped at
±64. Rating has a floor of 0; there are no tier floors protecting promotions.

Bronze starts at 0, Silver at 200, Gold at 500, Platinum at 900, High Court at
1,400. Badges are unranked until the fifth eligible match. Existing host-refereed
match eligibility still requires a final survivor, at least 15 seconds, two
accepted answers and two answering players. Host removals void the result.

## Research and tradeoffs

Epic's [Fortnite Ranked explanation](https://www.epicgames.com/help/c-34254770/a24421350)
uses placement and eliminations. EA's [Apex Ranked guide](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/apex-legends/ranked/)
describes placement, eliminations, bonuses and rank-dependent entry costs.
Those are useful examples of rewarding more than the final winner. Councillion
uses its own published placement table for a recurring group of friends rather
than copying matchmaking formulas intended for large competitive populations.

The user-requested +40/−18 duel baseline deliberately favors upward progress
at an even win rate. CR is a progression ladder, not a calibrated estimate of
competitive skill. Losses still demote immediately, and small capped bonuses
avoid the previous large differences between otherwise similar players.

## Earned stones and account titles

The original stone is free. Other stones need hard, distinct saved receipts:
25 substantial four-player wins; five flawless four-player wins; ten Daily
scores of 550+; five substantial six-player wins; or a seven-Krillion Daily.
Database rules verify every proof against that account's immutable records and
protect permanent unlock receipts. This inherits the game's existing trust
model: hosts referee multiplayer, and solo summaries are client reported.

`pres` and `vp` are optional display titles assigned to exact verified Google
emails. Turning them off changes appearance only, never permissions. The new
VP email remains a normal player account. Stone ownership also grants no role.
